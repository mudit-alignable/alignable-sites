What Steamboat Violin does

Steamboat Violin brings every genre to life with electric flair and heart! Carolyn, a Steamboat local, creates unforgettable moments for any event—weddings, parties, or venues—anywhere in Colorado and beyond. Pure music magic, every time! Hi, I’m Carolyn! 🎻 Welcome to my page. I am a dedicated electric violinist with a deep passion for connecting with people through the powerf music. My mission is simple: to bring soul-stirring live music to anyone and everywhere. Music has the incredible ability to transform an environment, lift spirits, and create unforgettable memories, and I bring that dedicated energy to every single performance. I specialize in creating custom, reimagined covers that bridge the gap between genres. From the sweeping elegance of traditional classical masterpieces to timeless radio classics and modern hits, my repertoire is designed to resonate with audiences of all backgrounds. I am completely versatile and highly adaptable, performing at a wide range of gatherings including weddings, private parties, corporate functions, cocktail hours, and community celebrations.
